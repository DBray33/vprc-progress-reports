EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

You pulled in 28 unique leads from website forms this month, with 123 spam submissions blocked automatically and 4 more reaching the inbox and flagged manually. Clicks came in at 186, up 5.7%, and total site users jumped to 1,019, up 77.8% over last month. Impressions dropped 17.4%, but that number is less alarming than it looks: the vast majority of lost impressions were deep-position, zero-click appearances that never drove traffic anyway, and rankings actually improved on hundreds of those same queries at the same time, so this is low-quality inventory falling off, not real visibility disappearing. On AI search, 35% of 612 prompt-platform combinations across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity mentioned Valley Peak, tested against 204 non-branded customer-style queries. The platform split tells the real story: Perplexity at 57% is strong, Gemini at 30% is solid, and ChatGPT at 18% is the clear gap to close. For next month, the priority is publishing two or three service-focused blog posts around storm damage, hail response, and pre-fall inspection topics, since August is peak season for that intent and we have the impressions to convert if the content matches what people are searching. Second, look at the 5 high-impression pages already sitting in positions 15 to 30 in Search Console and tighten up their title tags and intro copy to pull more clicks from traffic we are already showing up for.

THE BOTTOM LINE

July was a month of genuine wins where it counts most: the site generated 28 qualified leads and 41 keywords now rank at position #1, up +51.9% MoM, which is the strongest ranking achievement since launch. Total users surged to 1,019 (+77.8% MoM) and clicks from Google Search rose to 186 (+5.7% MoM) even as overall impressions pulled back, which tells us the site is getting more relevant exposure rather than just more exposure. The one area that needs attention is a cluster of high-value transactional keywords that dropped sharply this month, including "roof repairs in my area" and "roofing repair and installation," which represent real revenue intent and need page-level recovery work. For August, the priority is shoring up those declining service-intent pages, pushing CTR improvements on the 20 high-impression problem keywords across the 5 pages sitting in positions 15 to 30 in Search Console, and capitalizing on the AI visibility momentum where 2 leads already arrived via AI referral.

Why Platforms Differ

Each AI platform sources information differently, which is why the mention rates vary so much:

  • Gemini uses Google Search Grounding - it pulls live data from Google's local pack, organic results, and Maps. Strong Google rankings translate almost directly into Gemini visibility.
  • ChatGPT uses Bing-based web search and leans heavily on aggregator sites (Yelp, Angi, BBB, HomeAdvisor). It's more conservative about naming small local businesses unless they're prominent on those third-party platforms.
  • Perplexity mixes web search with citation-heavy sources, often surfacing aggregator pages first.

What this means for strategy: Gemini visibility is largely a downstream effect of Google rankings. Closing the ChatGPT gap requires building presence on third-party platforms - fully-built Yelp profile, claimed Angi listing, BBB accreditation, HomeAdvisor pro page, optimized Google Business Profile.
