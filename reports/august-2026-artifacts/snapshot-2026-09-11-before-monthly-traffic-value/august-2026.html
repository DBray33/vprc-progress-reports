EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This month, 24 unique leads came in from website forms. That is down from 28 in July but above the monthly average of 19 since January, which has climbed from 12 at the end of February. 180 spam submissions were blocked automatically and 5 more that reached the inbox were flagged manually. Clicks came in at 222, up 19.4% from last month, and average position improved by 3.3 spots to position 16.4, so the site is climbing where it counts. Impressions dropped 17.1%, and 94% of the lost impressions came from deep-position queries that never produced a single click, with rankings actually improving on 362 of those same queries at the same time. On AI search, 34% of 612 prompt-platform combinations mentioned Valley Peak across a 204-prompt non-branded customer-search test on ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Per platform: ChatGPT 16%, Gemini 39%, Perplexity 48%. ChatGPT is the clear gap to close. For September, the priorities are: refresh the existing winter roof prep guide with a built-in fall inspection checklist, along with the existing repair-or-wait and storm damage posts, before the pre-winter search wave picks up in October; refresh existing service pages with stronger trust signals like real project photos and FAQs; and work on improving click-through rate on 15 high-impression pages where we already rank but are not getting the tap.

THE BOTTOM LINE

August was a mixed month for Valley Peak Roofing. The site generated 24 qualified leads and organic clicks climbed +19.4% MoM even as total impressions pulled back. Leads declined month-over-month, dropping from 28 to 24, and that trend warrants attention. Average ranking position improved by 3.3 positions, and the tracked keyword list now shows 46 keywords at position 1, up +12.2%. The one area to watch is a cluster of high-value service keywords, including "roof repair services near me," that gave back significant ground this month; those pages need a focused refresh before fall demand peaks. Next month, the priority is converting the ranking momentum into stronger click-through rates on high-impression pages and refreshing the existing pre-winter posts early enough for Google to recrawl them before homeowners start searching.
