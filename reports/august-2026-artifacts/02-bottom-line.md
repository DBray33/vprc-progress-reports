# Bottom Line

<p>August was a mixed month for Valley Peak Roofing. The site generated <strong style="color: #BF5AF2;">24 qualified leads</strong> and organic clicks climbed <strong style="color: #34C759;">+19.4% MoM</strong> even as total impressions pulled back. Leads declined month-over-month, dropping from 28 to 24, and that trend warrants attention. Average ranking position improved by <strong style="color: #34C759;">3.3 positions</strong>, and the tracked keyword list now shows <strong>46 keywords at position 1</strong>, up <strong style="color: #34C759;">+12.2%</strong>. The one area to watch is a cluster of high-value service keywords, including "roof repair services near me," that gave back significant ground this month; those pages need a focused refresh before fall demand peaks. Next month, the priority is converting the ranking momentum into stronger click-through rates on high-impression pages and refreshing the existing pre-winter posts early enough for Google to recrawl them before homeowners start searching.</p>
