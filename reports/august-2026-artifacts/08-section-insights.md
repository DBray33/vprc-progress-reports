# Section Insights

## ga4

Users surged <strong>+79.2% MoM to 1,826</strong>, but engagement rate slipped <strong>5.5 percentage points</strong> to 31.5%, signaling that the new traffic volume is coming in colder and less intent-driven than last month's baseline. Focus on landing page relevance and clear calls to action to convert volume into leads.

## trafficSources

Paid Social is carrying <strong>999 of 2,024 sessions</strong> (nearly half of all traffic) but converting at a weak <strong>21.7% engagement rate</strong>, while Organic Search delivers only <strong>356 sessions</strong> yet engages at <strong>60.4%</strong>, nearly three times better. Organic is the quality channel; the overall engagement dip this month is a paid social dilution problem, not a site problem.

## topPages

The homepage dominates with <strong>138 clicks from 34,440 impressions at position 10.3</strong>, but the real standout is <strong>/our-company</strong>, which ranks at a sharp <strong>position 4.2</strong> and pulls <strong>10 clicks from just 458 impressions</strong>, the highest CTR efficiency in the top pages set. Blog content is also pulling weight, with the black-streaks post earning <strong>12 clicks at position 10.9</strong> from 2,557 impressions.

## localPack

Valley Peak holds <strong>10 Local Pack wins</strong>, which is a meaningful foothold in map-pack visibility for a site only 407 days old. Expanding Google Business Profile content, specifically fresh project photos and consistent review responses, is the fastest lever to grow that number before fall storm season peaks.

## keywords

There are <strong>9 problem pages</strong> sitting on high impressions with low clicks, representing the clearest near-term traffic opportunity in the dataset. These keywords are already earning Google visibility; tightening title tags and meta descriptions to match search intent on those pages could convert existing impressions into clicks without requiring new rankings.

## competitors

<strong>alankunsmanroofing.com</strong> is the competitor to watch most closely, sharing <strong>41 keywords</strong> with Valley Peak while ranking across <strong>102 total keywords</strong>, giving it the broadest overlap and the widest reach of any competitor in this set. <strong>homeshieldpros.com</strong> is a close second with <strong>34 shared keywords across 117 total</strong>, meaning it has more keyword range and likely stronger topical authority to close the gap on.

## competitorsWorthNoting

The same small group of local competitors keeps appearing across shared keywords, which confirms this is a defined, stable market rather than a fragmented one with dozens of aggressive players. Valley Peak already overlaps with the top two competitors on <strong>41 and 34 keywords respectively</strong>, meaning the site is already in the competitive conversation. The realistic opening is depth: competitors like <strong>ziggysroofing.us</strong> rank on only <strong>35 total keywords</strong>, so deepening the existing service pages (project proof, FAQs, internal links) could push Valley Peak past thinner competitors quickly.
