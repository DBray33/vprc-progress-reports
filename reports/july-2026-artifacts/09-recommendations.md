Together these two variants account for over 36,800 impressions but are splitting authority and confusing Google about which version to rank and display.

**Action:** 

**Impact:** Consolidating impression share under one URL and improving the meta description should lift homepage CTR from its current blended rate toward 2 to 4% within 60 days, which at 36,800 combined impressions would translate to roughly 370 to 740 additional clicks per month from queries where the site already ranks in the top two positions.

**Priority:** High
